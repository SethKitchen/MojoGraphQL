You need a commercial license if you use MojoGraphQL in connection with a business
or other commercial activity — shipping it inside a product you sell, using it
to render assets for a commercial project, or running it as part of a
revenue-generating service.

You do **not** need one for the purposes the noncommercial license already
grants, which include:

- Personal projects, hobby work, and private study
- Research, experimentation, and testing for public knowledge
- Use by a charitable organization, educational institution, public research
  organization, public safety or health organization, environmental protection
  organization, or government institution — regardless of how it is funded

Evaluating MojoGraphQL to decide whether to buy a commercial license is itself
noncommercial use. Try before you buy.
